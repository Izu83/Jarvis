How it works.

Jarvis lives on your Windows PC. Your phone and the desktop app are windows into him.

  1. He hears youThe wake word and speech recognition run on your PC. Nothing is sent anywhere to listen.
  2. Quick things first“Next song” or “lock the PC” is understood on the spot, with no AI. Instant and free.
  3. Claude plans the restBigger requests go to Claude, which uses Jarvis's tools step by step while you watch.
  4. He answers out loudIn a natural voice, on the PC or your phone, wherever you asked.

Private by design.

Your PC does the work. What leaves it is encrypted.

  • Your voice stays on your PCThe wake word and speech recognition both run locally.
  • End-to-end encryptedPhone and PC talk with AES-256. The tunnel in between only sees scrambled data.
  • Asks before risky thingsDeleting, running commands, shutting down: with Face ID on the phone, or Windows Hello on the PC.
  • Your keys stay yoursThey live only on your PC and your paired phone. The code and this site contain no secrets.
