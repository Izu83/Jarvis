Features

Everything, in one assistant.

Runs your PC.

Apps, windows across your monitors, music, volume. “Put Spotify and Discord on my left monitor.”

Hears his name.

“Hey Jarvis”, in English or Bulgarian. Say “stop” to cut him off.

Website jobs, out of sight.

In his own hidden browser. Your screen stays yours.

A morning briefing, reminders, and a heads-up when something matters.

Private by design.

Your PC does the work. What leaves it is encrypted.

Your voice stays home.The wake word and speech recognition run on your PC.
End-to-end encrypted.The tunnel between phone and PC only sees scrambled data.
Your keys stay yours.Only on your PC and your paired phone. This site holds no secrets.
Hard limits.Windows and your main folders can never be deleted. Deletes go to the Recycle Bin.
